Privacy Policy & Data Usage

How MedGuard protects your health data

Data Collection & Consent

At MedGuard, your privacy is our top priority. We collect health data (sleep, exercise, symptoms, etc.) only with your explicit consent. This data is used solely to provide personalized health insights and improve your experience on the platform.

Anonymized Analytics

Aggregated and anonymized data may be used for analytical purposes to help us understand broader health trends. Your personal identity (name, email) is never shared or linked to this anonymized data.

Data Portability

You have the right to export your health data at any time. We provide tools to download your history in standard formats (PDF/CSV) so you can share them with your healthcare provider.

Data Deletion

You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated health records at any time. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered.
